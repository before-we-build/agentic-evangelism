# Start without unnecessary spending

[Українська](../uk/costs.md) · [Русский](../ru/costs.md) · English

**Do not buy subscriptions just to try this.** Start with a recording you have permission to use and your own photos. This does not require Suno. Someone at church may be able to help with your first video.

## If you want a song from Suno

Checked September 12, 2026. Download rules changed on September 3.

| Option | Price and main limit |
| --- | --- |
| Free — try it | $0; up to 7 trial downloads over the lifetime of your account, for personal, non-commercial use only. |
| Pro — cheapest paid plan | Budget around $10 for a month with monthly billing; 20 song downloads per month. |

Free trial downloads do not renew monthly. Once used up, you can still listen and share links on Suno. [Download rules](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13614785).

On the pricing page, **$8/month means annual billing — $96 upfront**, before taxes. Choose Monthly for a first trial if you decide to pay. Pro includes 2,500 credits per month: these are for making music, separate from your download allowance. Check the final total before paying. [Pricing](https://suno.com/pricing) · [Monthly price help](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2425729).

## Can you publish it?

Not earning money does not by itself establish that a church channel counts as “personal use.” Ask Suno about permission for that channel, or use another recording you have permission to publish.

Current Suno help links commercial rights to official downloads made during a paid subscription. Older help pages describe different rules for Free songs; ask support to confirm the status of an older song. [Paid plan rights](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/9601665).

A subscription does not grant permission to use someone else's lyrics or music. Joint remixes on Suno have a separate non-commercial restriction even on paid plans. Do not bypass unavailable downloads by recording playback. [Suno terms](https://about.suno.com/terms).

## If money is tight

- Use your own song and recording, or obtain permission for the song, recording and video publication. Recording someone else's song yourself does not replace that permission.
- Use your own photos instead of paid image generation: [make a video](first-video.md).
- Start with available free Codex access within its limits: [costs and limitations](README.md). A free project does not mean unlimited AI services.
- If you need Suno Pro, you can pay for one month and cancel renewal before the next charge. Rights to permitted commercial downloads survive cancellation. [Subscription and rights](https://about.suno.com/terms).

Everyone in the youth group does not need to buy subscriptions. You can divide the work: someone writes lyrics, someone takes photos, and someone assembles the video. Account owners keep their passwords private.
